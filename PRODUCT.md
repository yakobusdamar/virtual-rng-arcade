# PRODUCT.md — Virtual RNG Arcade Simulator

## Users
Casual Indonesian web players who tap a slot-style machine out of curiosity; players who have seen real-money gambling apps (Gates-of-Olympus-style slots, "judol" ads) and are the target of education-by-parody. Mobile-first (390px), desktop secondary. No login, anonymous, local-only data.

## Product Purpose
An entertaining RNG arcade machine that gradually reveals the mathematics behind long-term losing probability. Slot-style fun on the outside, a transparent math experiment on the inside. Normal Mode = free playground; Challenge Mode = fixed, visible negative-EV odds with reality checks.

## Brand
Retro arcade cabinet + probability laboratory + satirical bank. Neon on deep cabinet blue, gold SIM COINS vs violet FUN POINTS, chunky retro shadows, Press Start 2P display type, lab-terminal stat panels, rubber stamps saying SIMULATION ONLY. Funny, playful, fictional. Never sexy-casino, never real gambling aesthetics' promise of wealth.

## Tone
Witty and self-aware. Wins are celebrated big precisely because they are fictional; the stats quietly tell the truth. Indonesian-flavored win lines, English lab-speak for numbers.

## Anti-references
- Real gambling platform look-and-feel that promises wealth: no Zeus gods, no "GATES OF OLYMPUS", no luxury gold-bar bonanza branding, no "casino felt" green.
- Real bank/payment branding (no logo lookalikes, no QRIS-style payment codes).
- SaaS dashboard clichés: hero-metric cards, glassmorphism everywhere, identical card grids.
- Miserable/lecturing tone; the app teaches by making the satire enjoyable.

## Strategic principles
1. Juice is the message: make spins feel amazing, then let the statistics panel tell you what it cost. Delight and honesty are not opposites.
2. Transparency is a feature: odds, EV, and reality checks are always one tap away, styled as lab readouts.
3. Two currencies never mix visually: gold 🪙 SIM COINS (credits) vs violet ⭐ FUN POINTS (score).
4. No real-money affordances anywhere: no buy/deposit/withdraw wording or affordances.
5. Mobile first, touch targets ≥ 44px, one-hand play.
