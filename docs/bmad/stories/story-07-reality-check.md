# Story 07 — Reality Check, Analytics, Balance Chart

## Story
As a player I want factual summaries of what Challenge Mode did to my balance, at honest milestones.

## Acceptance Criteria
1. Reality check modal after challenge spins reach 50 / 100 / 500 (once per threshold per lifetime until reset). [UT §19]
2. Modal shows: spin count, starting balance, total spent, total returned, NET RESULT, RETURN RATE (%) — computed, factual. [UT]
3. Witty closing line ("You didn't lose every spin. That's the clever part."). [GUI]
4. Balance history recorded after every challenge spin. [UT §24]
5. Recharts line chart of balance over time; wins green dots / losses red dots when ≤ 500 points; big-win spikes visible; long-term trend obvious. [GUI]
6. Chart accessible from Challenge stats tab. [GUI]
